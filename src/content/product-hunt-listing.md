## Maker Comment (First Comment)

**This is the most important part of the Product Hunt listing. Write it in first person. Be genuine, not salesy.**

> Hi Product Hunt! 👋
>
> Three months ago, I spent three weeks in Jumla, Nepal — one of the most remote places in the Himalayas. No grid power. No paved roads. No internet.
>
> What I found was a community that had everything they needed except the right tools.
>
> So we built an open-source "operating system" for remote communities. Five interconnected systems:
>
> ♻️ **Trash to Filament** — collect plastic waste, shred it, extrude it into 3D printer filament, print replacement parts
> 💧 **Water Purification** — pre-filter → membrane → UV → clean drinking water for 500+ people per node
> ⚡ **Energy Microgrid** — solar + hydro + battery, 24/7 power for a village (design targets 50kWh/day)
> 🍎 **Food Production** — cold storage, cider press, vinegar fermentation, dehydrator — 10x farmer income
> 🚗 **Logistics EV** — 50km EV corridor with charging stations, plus eVTOL cargo drone for emergency supply
> 📡 **Mesh Communication** — LoRa backbone spanning the valley, Wi-Fi clusters in villages, local LLM for offline AI assistance
>
> Every design is open source. Every module is documented with build guides, BOMs, and operator manuals. Hardware under CERN OHL-S. Software under AGPL-3.0. Docs under CC BY-SA 4.0.
>
> We also built a residency program (come live in Jumla for 3-12 months and build) and a remote accelerator (contribute from anywhere).
>
> This is v0.1. Far from finished. But it exists, it works, and it's free.
>
> Would love your thoughts, questions, and pull requests 🙏

## Supporter Recruitment

Identify 10-15 people who will upvote and leave genuine comments on launch day:

- Current maintainers and contributors (5-8 people)
- Friends of the project who have seen the work (3-5 people)
- Jumla community members who have internet access — their perspective is invaluable

**Do not ask for fake engagement.** Every supporter should have a real reason to care about the project.
