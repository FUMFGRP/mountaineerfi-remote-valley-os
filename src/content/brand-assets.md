## Brand Promise

**Peaks, not paperwork.**

## Brand Archetype

The Explorer. Adventurous, resilient, authentic, inspirational, respectful. Driven by the horizon, not the destination. A guide — never the guru.

## Colour System

### Primary

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Glacial Blue | `#A5D8FF` | 165 · 216 · 255 | Inspiration, clarity, perspective |
| Ice White | `#FFFFFF` | 255 · 255 · 255 | Purity, achievement, snow-capped peaks |
| Charcoal | `#2D3436` | 45 · 52 · 54 | Strength, resilience, mountain rock |

### Secondary

| Name | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Mountain Gray | `#636E72` | Supporting text, secondary elements |
| Snow Shadow | `#DFE6E9` | Secondary section backgrounds |
| Deep Blue | `#74B9FF` | Primary buttons, links |
| Sunset Orange | `#E17055` | Booking CTAs, warmth, action |

### Usage Rules

- **Primary text on Ice White:** Charcoal at 100% — body copy, navigation, labels
- **Headings:** Charcoal on light / Ice White on dark — never grey-on-grey
- **Accents:** Glacial Blue for calm, Sunset Orange for moments of warmth and action
- **Buttons:** Deep Blue (primary) · Glacial Blue (secondary). Sunset Orange for "Book" CTAs only
- **Backgrounds:** Ice White (main) · Snow Shadow (secondary sections)
- **Footers & deep moments:** Charcoal — used sparingly for emphasis

## Typography

### Primary: Montserrat
Clean, geometric sans for all reading — body, navigation, captions, data.
Weights: 300, 400, 600, 700 only. Avoid italic outside emphasis.

### Accent / Display: Caveat
Handwritten voice for moments that should feel like a journal entry.
Reserved for: logo, expedition titles, pull-quotes, signatures.
Never use for paragraphs.

### Type Scale

| Style | Font | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Headline | Caveat 700 | 64–144 px |
| Section title | Montserrat 600 | 32 px |
| Subheading | Montserrat 600 | 20 px |
| Body | Montserrat 400 | 16 px |
| Caption | Montserrat 300 | 13 px |
| Eyebrow | Montserrat 600 | 11 px, 0.22em tracking |

Never bring in a third family. The restraint is the point.

## Imagery

- **Authentic:** Real expeditions, named photographers. No stock libraries, no AI peaks.
- **Cinematic:** Wide frames showing scale — tiny humans against vast geography.
- **Natural:** Full colour, natural contrast, subtle vignettes. No heavy filters or presets.
- **Reverent:** Action over pose. Hands and gear over headshots. The mountain leads the composition.

Every frame should look like it was taken with a numb finger.

---

## Iconography

- Clean lines, rounded ends, no clever metaphors
- Stroke: 1.5–2 px on a 24 px grid
- Rounded caps and joins
- Limit each icon to a single concept

Available icons: Mountain, Compass, Ice Axe, Binoculars, Summit Flag, Elevation, Old Map, Journal, Snowflake, Sun, Wind, Vintage Camera

## Brand Hierarchy

```
Mountaineer.fi
├── Expeditions (lane 01) — permit & basecamp
├── Content Archive (lane 02) — articles, research, history
└── Remote Valley OS (lane 03) — open-source blueprint
```

Each lane has its own tone, but all share the same visual identity system.

---

## Do / Don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use authentic photography from real expeditions. Name the photographer. | Use clichéd stock imagery or AI-generated peaks. |
| Maintain generous whitespace. Let the layout breathe. | Use busy layouts that compromise readability. |
| Honour mountaineering history. Cite sources. | Alter historical facts, dates, or attributed achievements. |
| Highlight human stories — climbers, Sherpas, families. | Introduce a third typeface or invent colours outside the palette. |
| Pair Caveat and Montserrat — and nothing else. | Use trendy effects (drop shadows, gradients, blurs). |

## Remote Valley OS Sub-Brand

RV-OS uses the same colour system and typography but with distinct messaging:

- **Tagline:** Fork the Alps. Climb the Himalayas.
- **Principle:** Regenerative by design, open by default.
- **Voice:** Pragmatic engineer voice. Lowercase, technical, modular. Less manifesto, more README.
- **License mention:** Visible in all RV-OS materials (CERN OHL-S / AGPL-3.0 / CC BY-SA 4.0)
